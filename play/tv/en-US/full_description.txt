JellyVerse TV brings your Jellyfin library to the big screen with a modern, remote-first design.

• Immersive home: ambient backdrops that follow your focus, spotlight rotation, Continue Watching, Next Up and latest rows
• Movies and TV Shows grids with sorting and filters
• Detail pages with seasons, episodes, cast and recommendations
• Search with voice
• Smart player: remote seek with acceleration, trickplay thumbnails while scrubbing, chapters, skip intro, next-episode autoplay, audio/subtitle tracks, subtitle size & style, speed, quality, hardware/software decoding, playback stats
• Sign in with Jellyfin Quick Connect – no typing with the remote
• Live TV (IPTV): bring your own M3U / Xtream playlist, channel up/down zapping, channel list and now/next guide
• Continue Watching appears on your Android TV home screen
• Music for Navidrome: mixes, albums, playlists and a big-screen player with synced lyrics
• English and தமிழ் (Tamil) interface

JellyVerse TV is a client for Jellyfin servers you run yourself. It does not provide any content.

JellyVerse is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.
